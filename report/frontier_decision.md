# BUB1B-MVA frontier decision: no defensible new drug claim yet

Research decision, 24 October 2026. This addendum supersedes the earlier shortlist as a recommendation for what to fund next. It does not change the completed genome analyses. Research hypotheses only; no intervention reaches the child without the clinical team.

**I did not find an approved drug or combination that meets the requested standard.** This is a conclusion from this bounded review, not proof that no such treatment exists. Two biological openings survive the eight negative analyses, but neither has a verified drug-to-functional-rescue bridge. Calling either a treatment strategy today would repeat the error of joining plausible but untested edges.

The single first blocking result is **a functional defect of the exact allele products that can be corrected at physiological protein abundance**. The most informative inexpensive experiment is a controlled replacement assay comparing WT, N1002K and the exact 1–736 product, with division fidelity as the main endpoint and ciliation in sister wells. Its special value is to test whether the stop allele could supply useful protein *without readthrough*, while directly challenging the ciliary explanation of the congenital findings. It is a model discriminator, not a patient assay or a proof of clinical safety.

## A new branch in the disease model: interphase cilia

Published MVA fibroblast experiments show impaired primary-cilium formation. The proposed G0 mechanism is BubR1-dependent CDC20 turnover, APC/C–CDH1 activity and control of Dishevelled. DVL knockdown partly restored ciliation. One correction used transfer of chromosome 15, not precise repair of one variant. The paper describes cystic kidneys, not nephrocalcinosis. [PMID 21389084; DOI 10.1093/hmg/ddr090](https://doi.org/10.1093/hmg/ddr090).

A separate study implicates inappropriate interphase PLK1/KIF2A activity and shows partial ciliation rescue by KIF2A knockdown in MVA cells. Its PLK1-inhibitor rescue statement is explicitly supported by “data not shown”; it is not a quantified drug-rescue result. [PMID 25660017; DOI 10.1016/j.celrep.2015.01.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2015.01.003).

This gives a possible additional causal chain:

**Exact BUB1B dysfunction → impaired G0 ciliary function → altered renal calcium handling and development.**

The first arrow is untested for these variants. A separate mouse distal-convoluted/connecting-tubule culture study found reduced calcium transport after deciliation; flow still increased transport after cilia removal. That supplies a renal experiment, not proof of this child's disease mechanism. **No source found** establishing BUB1B-dependent hypercalciuria or nephrocalcinosis. The final disease-specific arrows remain hypotheses. [PMID 28100643; DOI 10.1096/fj.201600687RRR](https://doi.org/10.1096/fj.201600687RRR).

Cilia also regulate myoblast differentiation, and ciliary/Hedgehog responses vary across RMS models. This makes the axis relevant to muscle biology and simultaneously creates an oncology concern: ciliation cannot be treated as an unconditional antitumor endpoint. **No source found** assigning this child's muscle phenotype to ciliary dysfunction or showing that restoring cilia prevents RMS. [PMID 24927541; DOI 10.1073/pnas.1323265111](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1323265111).

This is published biology newly applied to the case, not a claim to have discovered a new BUB1B function. It could explain more than a purely mitotic model if demonstrated. It currently explains none of the unexplained findings conclusively.

## Fasudil is a concrete laboratory lead, but fails the treatment bar

Fasudil restored cilia incidence at 1 µM in human IFT88- and RPGRIP1L-heterozygous models; an RPGRIP1L knockdown model required at least 10 µM. TMEM67 models did not show comparable nontoxic rescue. RPGRIP1L-null kidney organoids had reduced cyst formation at 2.5 µM. No BUB1B model or calcium-deposition endpoint was tested. Thus ROCK inhibition is a testable bypass hypothesis, with **no source found** for the missing BUB1B bridge. [PMID 40253509; DOI 10.1038/s43856-025-00847-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43856-025-00847-1).

Current qualifying approval is verified: **Japan, PMDA, Eril Injection 30 mg, approval 21900AMX00715000**, for cerebral vasospasm and associated ischemic symptoms after subarachnoid-hemorrhage surgery. The April 2026 label describes adult IV treatment for approximately two weeks, discourages indefinite use and reports no pediatric clinical trials. Hypotension, bleeding and impaired renal clearance matter here. It supplies neither a chronic pediatric formulation nor a sustained free-exposure bridge to the culture experiments. [Official PMDA label, sections 4 and 6–9](https://www.pmda.go.jp/PmdaSearch/iyakuDetail/100898_2190414A1033_1_07). Regulatory labels have no PMID/DOI; none is invented.

There is relevant adverse mechanistic evidence. Fasudil/ROCK inhibition produced cytokinesis failure and other mitotic defects in BRCA2-deficient models, commonly at 64–128 µM. These concentrations and genotypes do **not** establish harm at 1 µM in BUB1B cells. They do rule out assuming that a ciliary benefit preserves chromosome fidelity. [DOI 10.7554/eLife.80254](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.80254). The [correction, DOI 10.7554/eLife.94414](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.94414), replaces a duplicated representative image; the authors state that its quantification and conclusions are unchanged.

**Decision:** do not nominate fasudil for chronic prevention or claim that it treats nephrocalcinosis. A future ciliary-rescue experiment must measure subsequent divisions and tumor-relevant effects, not count cilia alone. No source found establishing genomic, immune-surveillance or chronic pediatric safety for this purpose. An immunosuppressive substitute does not solve an unmeasured functional defect.

An oral alternative does not currently close the gap. Sertraline induced ciliation in RPE cells at 10 µM for 24 hours, without a BUB1B model. [PMID 25671433; DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0118190](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0118190). FDA-approved Zoloft has tablets and a 20 mg/mL oral solution, with pediatric OCD use at ages 6–17; appetite/weight, bleeding, hyponatremia and suicidality warnings matter. [Current official label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=fda754f6-d0f3-4dce-a17a-927d64f912f7). Historical labeled pediatric mean peaks were 165/123 ng/mL at the studied maximum dose: approximately 0.539/0.402 µM total, calculated with free-base molecular weight 306.23. The nominal ciliation concentration is 18.6–24.9 times those total peaks; neither culture binding nor relevant intracellular free exposure was established. [Historical official PK source](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=966d728e-87ca-4491-9411-2ec253eca632&type=display). This is an exposure gap, not evidence for dose escalation or a second treatment candidate.

## The overlooked stop-allele question has real counterevidence

Could supplying the short product restore a useful function without synthesizing full-length protein? In 2012, a 731X construct supported checkpoint and segregation assays; its quantified abundance was 1.44 relative to WT 1.00. [PMID 22698286; DOI 10.1016/j.devcel.2012.03.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.devcel.2012.03.009). A 2013 study found that 1–730, and a construct that directly targeted PP2A-B56 to kinetochores, improved chromosome alignment in MVA fibroblasts. Alignment is narrower than sustained fidelity. [PMID 23789096; DOI 10.1242/bio.20134051](https://doi.org/10.1242/bio.20134051).

**731X and 1–730 are the same truncation boundary, not two different patient alleles.** Neither is the child's exact 1–736 product. Crucially, a 2020 study also tested 731X and found deficient KARD phosphorylation, kinetochore PP2A-B56 recruitment and alignment/checkpoint-exit functions, including experiments with matched kinetochore abundance. This is substantive counterevidence; an expression difference is only a possible explanation, not a demonstrated reconciliation. [PMID 33207204; DOI 10.1016/j.celrep.2020.108397](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2020.108397).

The hypothesis therefore survives only as an experiment: **at a physiological dose, exact 1–736 might supply a useful function alongside N1002K**. The products might complement different residual functions even if neither is sufficient alone; **no source found** demonstrating that combination or the short product's benefit. It does not require N1002K destabilization or amino-acid insertion at the stop. It also does not justify selecting an NMD inhibitor before establishing whether the recovered product helps or harms.

Amlexanox illustrates the remaining pharmacological gap. Its published NMD/readthrough experiments involve other genes and nominal micromolar concentrations, not BUB1B fidelity rescue. [PMID 22938201; DOI 10.1186/1750-1172-7-58](https://doi.org/10.1186/1750-1172-7-58). US Aphthasol had approval for aphthous ulcers as a 5% dental paste; the current FDA record lists discontinued marketing. That is **not** proof of withdrawn approval, and a current systemic product was not verified here. [FDA record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/index.cfm?event=overview.process&ApplNo=020511), [historical approved label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2002/20511s002lbl.pdf). No source found for a safe, effective chronic pediatric BUB1B exposure. It is not the answer to this request.

Nor does genetic PP2A targeting justify a global phosphatase drug. Independent biochemical work did not substantiate perphenazine activation of reconstituted PP2A-B56; related proposed activators had other cellular effects. [PMID 35695070; DOI 10.15252/embj.2022110611](https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.2022110611). No approved selective kinetochore-restoration intervention was verified.

## The one experiment to fund first

**Question:** which of the exact allele products can support accurate division at a physiological dose, and does the same model have a separable G0 ciliary defect?

This is a proposed experiment, not an executed result. Use an existing ciliated diploid human line, such as RPE-1, with acute BUB1B depletion and controlled replacement. It avoids an initial animal model, kidney organoid or genome-wide screen. It is the lowest-cost informative design identified here if a laboratory already has the line and imaging system; no price or turnaround has been quoted. It cannot reproduce the child's endogenous RNA regulation or every tissue context.

Use two independent depletion reagents, or equivalent specificity evidence, and constructs that supply the exact coding products directly. Prefer untagged BubR1. Measure residual endogenous protein, replacement abundance and cell-to-cell heterogeneity. Match protein separately in G0 and mitosis; an average western blot must not conceal a mixture of null and overexpressing cells. The approximately half-normal targets below are experimental dosage controls, **not measured patient expression**. The adopted in-trans model is retained without reopening unavailable phasing.

| Condition after endogenous depletion | Decision it controls |
|---|---|
| Empty replacement | Establishes the depletion phenotype. |
| WT at normal abundance | Demonstrates that the assay detects a correctable BubR1 defect. |
| WT at approximately half-normal abundance | Separates reduced dosage from a variant-specific functional defect. |
| N1002K at that same abundance | Tests the exact missense product against the dosage control. |
| Exact 1–736 at that same abundance | Tests short-product function without endogenous NMD or readthrough as confounders. |
| N1002K plus 1–736, each at the half target | Tests the conditional protein state if the stop allele were recovered. |

Include an undepleted/nontargeting reference. Use a small measured dose bracket if a conclusion depends on one expression point. Add a no-depletion short-product arm if dominant interference is suspected. These additions resolve an ambiguity; they are not a license to search expression levels until a positive result appears.

Split sister wells between quiescence and cell-cycle re-entry. In G0, measure the ciliated fraction among viable quiescent cells, with membrane, axoneme and basal-body markers; verify comparable density and quiescence. CDC20/DVL and localized PLK1/KIF2A markers can distinguish the proposed pathways, but are secondary endpoints. In the re-entry arm, score the first divisions by blinded live imaging: segregation errors per completed division, failed cytokinesis, death, mitotic duration and completed-division output per starting cell. A fixed sister well can measure kinetochore B56 and KARD phosphorylation. Increased ciliation through arrest, or fewer abnormal survivors through selective killing, fails the joint-rescue claim.

The main falsifiable prediction is that adding physiological 1–736 to N1002K improves division fidelity relative to N1002K alone without reducing viable division output. The ciliary arm asks whether the proposed extra disease mechanism is present in this model; it is not presumed to be abnormal.

**Proposed decision rule:** first require a reproducible defect that WT corrects. For error rates, define gap closure as `(error_mutant − error_rescue) / (error_mutant − error_WT)`. Proceed only if closure is at least 50%, with a culture-level confidence interval excluding zero, and viable completed-division output is at least 90% of the matched mutant control. Report both effect and uncertainty. A new increase in failed cytokinesis, bridges or cell death prevents a favorable joint claim. For a subsequent ciliary drug test, an illustrative harm gate is an upper confidence bound below a 2-percentage-point increase in abnormal/failed divisions; lack of statistical significance alone is not a pass.

These numbers are author-selected laboratory gates, **not validated clinical thresholds**. No source found validating them as surrogates for cancer prevention, growth or renal benefit. Independent culture runs are the biological replicates; cells and wells are nested observations. Pilot variability determines the required replication. Inadequate precision means unresolved, not safe.

| Result | What it permits |
|---|---|
| WT fails to rescue the baseline | Stop interpretation; repair the assay. A negative mutant result is uninformative. |
| Exact 1–736 fails across the measured dose bracket while WT works | Reject the NMD-only short-product premise in this model. Do not promote an NMD drug from the older truncation papers. |
| 1–736 improves fidelity with preserved output | Prioritize endogenous exact-genotype validation and RNA recovery/exposure work. This supplies no approved-drug or clinical-safety claim by itself. |
| N1002K differs from half-dose WT in ciliation, and WT corrects the deficit | Justifies a later ciliary bypass experiment. It does not explain nephrocalcinosis until a renal-lineage calcium-transport defect is demonstrated. |
| Both products look normal in these endpoints | The model has not captured the disease mechanism. Do not declare the variants harmless. |

A no-drug replacement experiment cannot falsify fasudil rescue. If a WT-correctable ciliary defect is established, a subsequent fasudil-versus-vehicle experiment can test it at measured exposure, with re-entry fidelity and appropriate positive controls. Failure rejects that model and exposure window. Success still requires endogenous genotype confirmation, renal transport, tumor counter-screens and a chronic pediatric exposure/safety case. No immunosuppressive, mutagenic or aneuploidy-promoting benefit is accepted as preventive rescue.

## What the three clues change

**Congenital nephrocalcinosis** makes a developmental/tubular function worth testing, but does not establish ciliary causation. A review of existing renal chemistry by the treating team could discriminate an independent tubulopathy; this is an information gap, not a diagnosis or authorization to contact anyone. Congenital onset alone also does not logically exclude damage beginning during embryonic divisions.

**The chr8p contrast** remains the supplied unconfirmed, method-sensitive result. It receives no causal weight in this decision, and no tissue clone, driver gene or drug target is inferred from it. See the completed [low-pass evidence and limitations](../results/lowpass_methods.md).

**The family-history term** (withheld from the public copy) does not identify a parental genotype or distinguish these rescue mechanisms. No new parental inference is made.

## Dependence on the eight failures

**None needs to be wrong.** The experiment asks about functions that stability scores, pockets, signature correlations and bulk blood copy-number contrasts do not measure. It requires no modifier, PGx advantage or corresponding mouse allele. It does not use arimoclomol, azithromycin, niacin or ataluren. It accepts the in-trans model for the protein-replacement comparison and does not seek unavailable phase information.

No new strategy here has two independent positive rescue lines, and no drug meets clinical eligibility. The justified advance is a narrower, falsifiable biological decision. The immediate bottleneck is the exact-genotype functional phenotype; even a successful experiment would leave pharmacology, oncology and clinical translation to solve.
